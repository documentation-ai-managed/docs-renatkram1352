## Overview

Project docs are organized by pages. Add new pages at any time.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your project">
    Go to your dashboard and select a project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Add Page'">
    Find the "Add Page" button in the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your page">
    Enter a clear title (e.g., `API Reference` or `Setup Guide`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start writing">
    Add content in markdown format, then click "Save".
  </Step>
</Steps>

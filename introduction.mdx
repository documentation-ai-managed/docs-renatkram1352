## Overview

renatkram1352 provides a centralized space for you to organize, create, and manage all your project documentation. You can build structured docs, collaborate with your team, and keep everything up-to-date without hassle. This default documentation workspace scales with your projects, from small prototypes to large-scale applications.

## Quick Start

Get up and running in minutes with these essential steps.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Space" icon="plus">
    Sign up at `https://dashboard.example.com` and initialize your renatkram1352 workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Your First Page" icon="file-plus">
    Click "New Page" and start writing in MDX. Use the editor for live previews.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organize Content" icon="layers">
    Drag and drop pages into folders. Add frontmatter for metadata like titles and descriptions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish and Share" icon="globe">
    Deploy your site and share the link with your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>
